# User Research

## Food Delivery Mobile App UX Design

### Research Objective

The objective of the user research was to understand
common user needs, expectations, and difficulties when
ordering food through mobile applications.

## Key User Needs

- Simple and easy navigation
- Quick food and restaurant discovery
- Clear restaurant and food information
- Transparent pricing and additional charges
- Easy food customization
- Simple cart and checkout process
- Multiple payment options
- Clear order tracking

## User Pain Points

- Too many food and restaurant choices
- Unclear additional charges
- Confusing food customization options
- Long or complicated checkout process
- Unclear delivery status
- Difficulty comparing restaurants and prices

### Design Requirements

Based on the research findings, the following
requirements were identified:
- Create simple and clear navigation
- Provide search and food categories
- Display restaurant ratings, cuisine, price, and delivery time
- Make food selection and cart management easy
- Provide Clear order summary and total cost
- Offer multiple payment options
- Provide clear order confirmation and delivery tracking

### Research Outcome

The research helped identify the main challenges users may face
during food ordering. These findings were used to guide the
persona, journey map, wireframes, UI design, prototype, and
usability testing for the project.
