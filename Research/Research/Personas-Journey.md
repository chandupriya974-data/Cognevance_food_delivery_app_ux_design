# User Persona and Journey Map

## Food Delivery Mobile App UX Design

## User Persona

**Name:** Ananya Sree
**Age:** 22
**Occupation:** College Student
**Location:** Bengaluru, India

### Bio

A busy college student who frequently orders
food through mobile apps. She prefers affordable
meals, quick delivery, easy navigation, and simple
payment options.

### Goals

- Find food quickly
- Compare restaurants and prices
- See accurate delivery times
- Customize food easily
- Complete orders quickly

### Needs

- Simple search and categories
- Clear restaurant information
- Easy cart and checkout
- Multiple payment options
- Real-time order tracking

### Pain Points

- Too many choices
- Unclear additional charges
- Confusing customaization
- Lengthy checkout process
- Unclear delivery status

### Motivations

- Convenience
- Affordable food
- Fast delivery
- Easy ordering experience



## User Journey Map

### 1. Discover
The User opens the app and searches 
for food or browses food categories.

## 2. Select
The User compares restaurants and
selects a restaurant and food item.

### 3. Customize
The user reviews the food details and
makes any required selections.

### 4. Cart
The user reviews selected items,
quantities, and the total amount.

### 5. Payment
The user selects a delivery address
and preferred payment method and places
the order.

### 6. Track Order
The user views the order confirmation
and tracks the delivery status.

### Journey Flow
**Discover --> Select --> Customize --> Cart --> Payment --> Track order**

### Design Insights
The journey map helped identify important moments where
the app should provide clear information and simple actions. 
These insights were used to design the navigation, food selection,
cart, checkout, payment, and order tracking experience.
