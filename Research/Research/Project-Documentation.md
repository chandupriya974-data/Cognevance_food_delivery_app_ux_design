# Food Delivery Mobile App UX Design

## Cognevance Technologies - Level 2 Intermediate Task

### 1. Project Overview

The Food Delivery Mobile App UX Design project focuses 
on creating a simple, convenient, and user-friendly mobile
experience for ordering food from nearby restaurants. The
design covers the complete journey from onboarding and restaurant
discovery to food selection, cart, payment, order confirmation, and tracking.

### 2. Project Objective

The objective of this project was to design a complete food 
delivery mobile app experience that is easy to navigate and
understand. The design aims to help users quickly discover 
food, select items, manage their cart, choose a payment
method, place an order, and track its delivery status.

### 3. Design Process

The project followed a user-centered UX design process:

- User Research
- Identifying User pain points
- User Persona
- User Journey Map
- Low-Fidelity Wireframes
- High-Fidelity UI Design
- Interactive Prototype
- Usability Testing

  ### 4. User Research

  User research was conducted to understand common
  challenges faced by people when ordering food through
  mobile apps.

  key pain points identified included:

  - Too many choices
  - Unclear additional charges
  - Confusing food customization
  - Lengthy checkout processes
  - Unclear delivery status

    Based on these findings, the design requirements focused
    on simple navigation, clear restaurant and food information,
    an easy cart and checkout process, multiple payment options,
    and clear order tracking.

    ### 5. User Persona

    **Name:** Ananya Sree
    **Age:** 22
    **Occupation:** College Student
    **Location:** Bengaluru, India

    **Bio:**
    A busy college student who frequently orders food
    through mobile apps and prefers affordable meals, quick
    delivery, easy navigation, and simple payment.

    **Goals:**
    - Find food quickly
    - Compare restaurants and prices
    - see accurate delivery times
    - Customize food
    - Complete orders quickly

      **Pain Points:**
      - Too many choices
      - Unclear additional charges
      - Confusing customization
      - Lengthy checkout
      - Unclear delivery status
     
      ### 6. User Journey

      **Discover --> Select --> Customize --> Cart --> Payment --> Track order**

      The journey begins with discovering food and restaurents,
      followed by selecting a restaurant and food item, customizing the
      order, reviewing the cart, making payment, and tracking the order until
      delivery.

      ### 7. Wireframes

      Low-fidelity wireframes were created for:

      - Onboarding
      - Home
      - Restaurant Details
      - Cart
      - Checkout & Payment
      - Order Confirmation & Tracking

        The wireframes helped establish the layout, content hierarchy, and
        main user flow creating the final UI.

        ### 8. High-Fidelity UI Design

        The low-fidelity wireframes were transformed into
        high-fidelity mobile app screens using Figma.

        The final UI design uses:

        - White background
        - Orange as the primary color
        - Rounded corners
        - Clear typography
        - Food imagery
        - Simple navigation
        - Organized information
       
        The design focuses on providing a simple and convenient
        food-ordering experience.

        ### 9. Prototype & Interactions

        An interactive prototype was created in Figma
        to demonstrate the complete food-ordering flow.

        **Main Flow:**

        Onboarding --> Home --> Restaurant Details --> Cart -->
        Checkout & Payment --> Order confirmation & Tracking

        The prototype allows users to navigate through the main
        ordering journey and interact with important actions such
        as get started, Add to cart, Proceed to Checkout, and Place
        Order.

        ### 10. Onboarding, Cart & Payment Flow

        The onboarding screen introduces the app and guides
        users to the home screen.

        Users Can:

        1. Browse restaurants and food categories
        2. Select food items
        3. Add items to the cart
        4. Review the order
        5. Choose a payment method
        6. Place the order
        7. View order confirmation
        8. Track the delivery status
       
        ### 11. Usability Testing

        A self-usability test was conducted to review the complete
        food-ordering experience.

        The ordering flow was tested from onboarding to order
        confirmation. The review focused on navigation, button
        visibility, information clarity, cart and checkout flow,
        payment options, and order tracking.

        The testing helped identify areas where navigation and visual
        clarity could be improved.
